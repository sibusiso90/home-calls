**Run by:** ______________________ (phone: ______________________)

I agree that Fade Call may:

- [ ] Take photos of my face and hair before and after a haircut, using the app's live camera.
- [ ] Record my voice in a voice note (barber profile or client complaint).
- [ ] Send these photos and voice notes to Meta's AI models (Muse Spark, Muse Voice Transcribe and SAM 3.1) to check the haircut, compare the before and after photos of the same job, describe my face shape and hairline to suggest cuts, and turn a voice note into text.

- Apart from the demo set I agree to above, raw photos are deleted after the job is checked.
- I can withdraw before the video is submitted (deadline 4 November 2026, 15:00 SAST) and my footage will be removed. After submission I can still ask for my data in the app to be deleted.
